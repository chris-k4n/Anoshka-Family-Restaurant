Our Story

A table in the heart of Margao

Anoshka began with a simple idea — that a meal should feel like a place, not just a plate. Every dish on our table draws from Goa's flavours and local markets, prepared with a quiet, deliberate hand.

Our dining room is designed to let the food take centre stage. It's a space for long dinners, golden evenings and relaxed conversations.

Four ways to spend an evening at Anoshka — choose the one that fits the occasion.

01

02

A closed room for intimate celebrations, curated menus and a dedicated host for the evening.

Chef's Table

A front-row seat to the kitchen, with a menu built and narrated by the chef, course by course.
