Our Story & Heritage

A table rooted in the soul of Goa

Anoshka was founded on the belief that dining is an art of gathering, where timeless recipes meet warm Goan hospitality.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen is a celebration of coastal tradition — from fresh catches brought in daily by local fisherfolk to fragrant spices ground with patient care. Whether you are seeking an unhurried family celebration or an intimate candlelit dinner, our doors welcome you like family.
